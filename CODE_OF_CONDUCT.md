# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and sexualized conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

Assume good intent while reviewing ideas rigorously. Keep feedback about the
work, protect private information, and respect exam integrity: do not solicit or
share confidential certification content.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
conduct is unsafe or persistently disruptive. Report conduct concerns through
the repository’s private security/contact channel.
