# Warranty Policy

## Coverage
- All products come with a 12-month manufacturer warranty from date of purchase.
- Warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.
- Warranty does NOT cover damage from misuse, accidents, or unauthorized modifications.

## Claims Process
1. Contact support with order number and description of the defect.
2. Provide photos or video of the defect.
3. We may request the item be returned for inspection.
4. Approved claims result in repair, replacement, or refund at our discretion.

## Extended Warranty
- Available for purchase within 30 days of the original order.
- Extends coverage to 24 months total.
- Cost: 15% of the original item price.
