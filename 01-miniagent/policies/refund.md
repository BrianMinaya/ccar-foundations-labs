# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
- Items purchased within the last 30 days are eligible for a full refund.
- Items purchased 31–60 days ago are eligible for store credit only.
- Items older than 60 days are not eligible for any refund.

## Process
1. Customer must provide order number and reason for return.
2. Refund is processed within 5–7 business days after item is received.
3. Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the item was defective.

## Exceptions
- Sale items are final sale and cannot be refunded.
- Digital products can only be refunded if not downloaded/accessed.
- Gift cards are non-refundable.
