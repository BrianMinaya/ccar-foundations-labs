# Shipping Policy

## Domestic Shipping
- Standard shipping: 5–7 business days, free on orders over £50.
- Express shipping: 2–3 business days, £7.99.
- Next-day shipping: 1 business day, £14.99.

## International Shipping
- Standard international: 10–15 business days, £12.99.
- Express international: 5–7 business days, £24.99.
- Not available to all countries. See website for list.

## Tracking
- All orders include tracking. Tracking number is emailed within 24 hours of dispatch.
- For delays beyond the estimated window, contact support with your order number.

## Lost or Damaged Shipments
- Report within 14 days of expected delivery date.
- Replacement or refund issued at our discretion.
